Cloud repair attestation · phones · cars · appliances

PROOF
OF REPAIR

Workshops seal what was fixed, when, and on which device: IMEI, VIN or serial number. A fine-tuned LLM writes the report from real repair manuals; the record is hashed, stored in PostgreSQL and anchored on Ethereum and Bitcoin (OpenTimestamps).

00 · Buyer / dealer lookup

Paste an IMEI, VIN or appliance serial number to see sealed repairs and whether they were anchored on-chain.

No sealed repairs in the registry for a device = nothing to trust yet.

01 · Workshop bay

Technician records a repair: pick the device type, describe the fault, and seal the report to the device identifier.

First run loads Mistral-7B (~20 s); each diagnosis then takes ~15–40 s on Apple Silicon.
